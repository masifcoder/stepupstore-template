The Pure Wholecut Oxford

Mastering the Wholecut is considered the zenith of footwear craftsmanship. Requiring a completely unblemished, continuous hide of full-grain calfskin, it offers no room for error. The shoe wraps around the foot like a bespoke glove, adapting to your foot’s unique contours over time.

Key Features:
  • Single piece unbroken upper construction without quarters or seams.
  • Goodyear welted closed-channel sole allowing indefinite resoling.
  • Hand-beveled waist and stacked leather heel with non-slip dovetail rubber insert.
  • Treated with natural beeswax and organic oils for supreme water repellency.
Fitting Advice

Standard 'F' fitting (Regular width). Excellent instep curvature suited for South Asian feet.

Recommend wearing with fine dress socks.
