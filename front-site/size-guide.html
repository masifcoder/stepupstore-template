How To Measure Your Foot
  1. Step onto Paper: Place a blank sheet of A4 paper on a hard, flat floor against a flat wall.
  2. Stand with Socks: Wear the typical socks you plan to wear with your dress shoes or loafers. Place your heel firmly touching the wall.
  3. Mark the Longest Point: Use a pen held vertically to mark the tip of your longest toe (usually the big toe or second toe).
  4. Measure with Ruler: Measure the distance from the edge of the paper to your mark in centimeters (cm).
  5. Match to Chart: Compare your measurement to our chart above. If you are between two sizes, choose the larger size for maximum comfort.
Wide Feet Advice

Gentlemen with wider feet or higher insteps frequently prefer our Plain Toe Derbies or Monk Straps which allow variable buckle and lacing expansion.
